Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

1. Overview

Xypher is a desktop application designed to help users organize and manage their own Roblox accounts and Roblox sessions.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Xypher may access, how that information is used, and how it is protected.

2. Information We Access

When you connect a Roblox account through Roblox OAuth, Xypher may receive information provided by Roblox that is necessary to identify the connected account.

Depending on the permissions granted through Roblox, this may include information such as:

  • Roblox account identifier
  • Roblox username
  • Display name
  • Basic profile information
  • OAuth authentication information

3. How Information Is Used

Information accessed through Roblox OAuth is used solely to provide the functionality of Xypher, including identifying connected accounts and managing the user's local account configuration.

Xypher does not sell personal information or use connected Roblox account information for advertising.

4. Local Storage

Xypher may store account-related information locally on the user's computer so that connected accounts remain available between application sessions.

Authentication information intended to remain private is stored using operating-system security mechanisms where supported.

5. Roblox Authentication

Xypher uses Roblox's OAuth authorization system when connecting Roblox accounts. Users authenticate directly with Roblox rather than providing their Roblox password to Xypher.

Xypher does not request or intentionally store Roblox account passwords.

6. Information Sharing

Xypher does not sell, rent, or intentionally share connected account information with third parties except where necessary to provide the application's requested functionality or where required by law.

7. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect locally stored authentication information and account configuration. However, no software or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Removal

Users may remove connected accounts from Xypher. Removing an account from Xypher removes its locally stored account configuration and associated local authentication information where applicable.

9. Children's Privacy

Xypher does not knowingly collect personal information from children for advertising or marketing purposes. Users should only connect accounts they are authorized to use.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as Xypher develops or its functionality changes. The latest version will be made available on this page.

11. Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be directed to the Xypher project owner through the project's official repository.
